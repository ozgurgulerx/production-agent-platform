# Escalation policy — v1, effective 1 Mar 2026

- Escalate to a human when the customer mentions a complaint to the regulator, bereavement, or financial hardship.
- Escalate when a refund is requested outside policy and the customer disagrees with the decision.
- Vulnerable-customer flags on the account mean a human handles the whole conversation.
- Target first response for escalations: 4 business hours.
