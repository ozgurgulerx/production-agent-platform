# Card policy (UK card team) — v2, effective 1 Jul 2026

- A lost or stolen card is blocked immediately on report; a replacement ships within 3 working days.
- Temporary freeze and unfreeze are self-service in the app and do not need an agent.
- Card limits can be raised by an agent up to £5,000; above that requires a credit review.
- Never ask the customer for the full card number, PIN or CVV; identity is verified by the app challenge.
- Travel notices are no longer required since May 2026.
