# Refund policy (UK card team) — v3, effective 1 Sep 2026

- Full refund within 14 days of purchase for unused digital goods; 30 days for physical goods returned unopened.
- Refunds above £250 require a second approver; the agent must not issue them alone.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method only. No cash, no alternative cards.
- Disputed transactions (chargebacks) are handled by the disputes team, not by refunds.
- Goodwill credits are capped at £25 per customer per quarter.
